---

### Conversation 2: Specific Calculation
**You:** "How much CO2 do I save by biking instead of driving to work (10km daily)?"

**EcoChat:** "Excellent choice! 🚴 Here's the calculation:

**Driving (Petrol Car):**
- Distance: 10 km
- Emission factor: 0.192 kg CO2/km
- Daily: 10 × 0.192 = 1.92 kg CO2
- Weekly (5 days): 9.6 kg CO2
- Yearly: 499.2 kg CO2

**Biking:**
- Emissions: 0 kg CO2 ✅

**You Save:**
- Daily: 1.92 kg CO2
- Weekly: 9.6 kg CO2
- Monthly: 41.6 kg CO2
- Yearly: 499.2 kg CO2

**Tree Equivalent:** That's like planting 23.8 trees! 🌳

**Bonus Benefits:**
- Save ~$1,200/year on fuel
- Burn 300 calories/commute
- Reduce traffic congestion

Ready to log this in the tracker?"

---

### Conversation 3: Waste Disposal
**You:** "I have old paint cans. How do I dispose of them?"

**EcoChat:** "Paint requires special handling. Here's what to do:

**If Paint is Liquid:**
1. ❌ NEVER pour down drain
2. Mix with kitty litter or sawdust to solidify
3. Let dry completely
4. Dispose as hazardous waste

**If Paint is Dried:**
1. Can go in regular trash (in most areas)
2. Remove lid to show it's dry
3. Check local regulations

**Best Option:**
Take to hazardous waste facility or paint recycling center

**Find Locations:**
- Earth911.com
- Your city's waste management website
- Hardware stores (some accept old paint)

**Eco-Tip:** Next time, buy only what you need to minimize waste! 🎨"

## 🧠 AI Capabilities

### What EcoChat CAN Do

✅ **Answer Questions** - General sustainability topics
✅ **Calculate Carbon** - Emissions for activities
✅ **Compare Options** - Best eco-friendly choices
✅ **Provide Tips** - Actionable advice
✅ **Explain Concepts** - How recycling works, etc.
✅ **Suggest Alternatives** - Eco-friendly swaps
✅ **Context Awareness** - Remembers conversation

### What EcoChat CANNOT Do

❌ **Real-Time Data** - Can't check live prices, weather
❌ **Personal Data Access** - Doesn't see your tracker data (for privacy)
❌ **Execute Actions** - Can't log activities for you
❌ **Local Specifics** - May not know your city's recycling rules
❌ **Medical Advice** - Stick to environmental topics
